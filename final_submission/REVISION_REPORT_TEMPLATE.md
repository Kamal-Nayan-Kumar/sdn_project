# Revision report template

Fill this in after the review round. The reviewer comments are pasted from
Google Deep Review by a human; the **action taken** and the **location in the
paper** are filled in by the author, and the paper is then recompiled with
`tectonic` and a new PDF is produced.

Keep one table per review round. Number the comments so they can be referred
to in the response letter.
